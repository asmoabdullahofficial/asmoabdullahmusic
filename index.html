About Me

I document reality.

ASMO ABDULLAH is a rapper and lyricist from Bangladesh, now living in Muscat, Oman. He started rapping at 17, stepped back to rethink his direction, and came back with what he calls Conscious Rap.

His songs speak about real struggles, the daily "9 to 5" grind, ambition, heartbreak and honest emotions like Icche. Alongside music, he works as a visual artist, building stories that people can hear and see.

"I don't just write rhymes; I document reality."
